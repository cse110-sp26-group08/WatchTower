Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

WatchTower helps developers monitor application errors, uptime, and performance. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as username and email address.
  • Application names, URLs, and API keys used to connect monitored apps.
  • Error, uptime, and performance metrics sent by the collector.

How We Use Information

WatchTower uses collected information to provide monitoring features, display application health data, notify users about issues, and improve platform reliability.

Data Security

WatchTower takes reasonable steps to protect account and monitoring data. Users should avoid sending sensitive personal data through application logs.

Third-Party Services

WatchTower may rely on third-party infrastructure, hosting, database, and email services to operate the platform.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the WatchTower team.
